# Demo scope (frozen)

Changing anything here means rewriting questions and checklists, so the whole team must agree first.

## 10 demo skills and their sub-skills

| Skill | Sub-skills |
| --- | --- |
| `sk_stitching` Stitching and tailoring | `ss_stitch_measure` taking measurements · `ss_stitch_cutting` cutting a pattern · `ss_stitch_machine` machine work and seams · `ss_stitch_fitting` fitting and alteration |
| `sk_drawing` Drawing and design | `ss_draw_proportion` shape and proportion · `ss_draw_shading` shading and detail · `ss_draw_colour` colour choice |
| `sk_electrical_basic` Basic electrical work | `ss_elec_safety` electrical safety · `ss_elec_circuits` simple circuits and switch wiring · `ss_elec_tools` tester and multimeter · `ss_elec_faults` finding faults |
| `sk_money_handling` Handling money | `ss_money_counting` totals and change · `ss_money_records` keeping accounts · `ss_money_pricing` cost, price and profit |
| `sk_selling` Selling and customers | `ss_sell_customer` handling customers · `ss_sell_explain` explaining a product · `ss_sell_negotiate` agreeing a price |
| `sk_cooking` Cooking | `ss_cook_hygiene` food hygiene · `ss_cook_quantity` quantities and recipes · `ss_cook_costing` costing a dish |
| `sk_computer_basic` Phone and computer use | `ss_digital_apps` using apps and UPI · `ss_digital_safety` spotting scams · `ss_digital_info` finding information and filling forms |
| `sk_crop_farming` Growing crops | `ss_farm_soil_water` soil and water · `ss_farm_pests` pests and plant health · `ss_farm_planning` season and crop planning |
| `sk_caring_people` Caring for people | `ss_care_firstaid` first aid · `ss_care_hygiene` hygiene and infection · `ss_care_listening` listening and reassuring |
| `sk_teaching_explaining` Teaching and explaining | `ss_teach_explain` explaining step by step · `ss_teach_check` checking understanding · `ss_teach_plan` planning a short lesson |

33 sub-skills in total. The other 15 skills in `packages/kb/skills.json` stay in the knowledge base. They can be self-reported and rated by teachers, but they have no question bank in the demo.
