# Who does what in Disha

**Principle:** Disha's fixed rules decide. The AI only reads open-ended input and suggests, and every AI output is checked by a rule before it is used. People confirm what only people can (seeing an original document, watching a student work, a parent's consent). The same list is in the app: Account → Who does what.

| | Does | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Disha (fixed rules)** | Skill level 1–4 per sub-skill from evidence (`formula.tracing`); career score Fit + Reach + Worth; scheme eligibility (JsonLogic); job readiness (`rule.readiness`); documents an application needs (`rule.apply_docs`); which course lessons are open (`rule.course_access`) and which courses to suggest (`rule.course_recommend`); is it really her in a task video (`rule.identity`); system-confirms clear videos and sends doubtful ones plus a random 10% to the teacher (`rule.autoconfirm`); donor pay check (`rule.paycheck`); payment signature check (`guard.payment_signature`); consent scopes, school-only data, unreadable phone numbers. | Decides anything about a person from AI output alone. |
| **AI helper** | Understands Tamil/Hindi/English speech and text; scores work photos/videos as one rater weighted by its agreement with teachers; reads resumes and takes a first look at documents; suggests careers from the catalogue; explains results in her words; speaks prompts aloud for phones with no Tamil voice. | Verifies a document, unlocks a lesson, releases money, or uses anything a rule has not checked. |
| **Teacher** | Confirms or corrects AI scores and spot-checks system-confirmed videos; rates skills and may override a level with a written reason; verifies documents after seeing the original and records the income on an income certificate; suggests internships, jobs and schemes with a note; posts jobs for the class; makes courses (free, pay per lesson, subscription) and earns 90% of fees; keeps the parent's signed paper consent form; keeps a public teacher profile. | A raised level never counts as proof for a donor payment; sees only their own school's students. |
| **Student and family** | Answer questions (voice, text or picture cards), take tests, film tasks or upload photos for any skill with a checklist, take courses, build a resume, upload documents, apply; choose what to share and can withdraw or delete at any time. | Has to share caste, income or a phone number with employers or donors. |
| **Donor** | Funds skill milestones; sees an anonymous pay check per student and payouts on the chain. Money moves only when every check passes. | Sees a student's name. |
| **Employer / customer** | Posts jobs and internships; moves applications on (seen, shortlisted, not selected, hired). A customer confirms a paid job with three answers after agreeing to the privacy notice. | Gets a student's phone number; meetings go through the school. |
